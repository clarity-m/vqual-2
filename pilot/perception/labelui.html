O C Utoggle occluded / clipped / unsure. Applies to the selected quad; while a quad is in progress it arms the flag for that quad instead.

QPnP snap: replace the selected quad's corners with the nearest exact square projection (gate is a known 1.5 m square). With exactly 3 corners placed, solves the 4th instead (hollow dot until you touch it); 3 corners allow 2–4 valid squares, so press Q again to cycle the candidates and keep the one on the gate. Skip snapping when the thing you're labelling isn't a clean square in view — your clicks win.
Zrevert the last snap (one level)
[ / ]move selected quad back / forward in z (later = in front)
+ / -zoom in / out (or mouse wheel over the image). Above 1x the step is 0.5, up to 12x. Below 1x it steps out through 0.75x → 0.5x → 0.35x → 0.25x — zoom OUT to reach amodal corners that sit far outside the image; at 0.25x the whole clickable margin (±1400 px around the frame) fits on screen at once.

AMODAL: when a gate is hidden by another gate, by the cyan ribbon, or by the image edge, click where the corner WOULD be — including outside the image, on the grey margin. Those coordinates are stored unclamped. Flag it O and/or C. For a frame-filling gate whose corners land far off the image, press - down to 0.5x or 0.25x first: the dashed white rectangle still marks the real 640×360 frame, and the corner dots stay the same size on screen however far you zoom out. Winding is normalised for you (clockwise from the top-left corner), so click the four corners in any rotational order as long as you go around the quad, not across it — a quad that comes out crossed is drawn red with a "!". Label back-to-front so z comes out right.
