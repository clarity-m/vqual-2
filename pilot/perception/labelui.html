AMODAL: when a gate is hidden by another gate, by the cyan ribbon, or by the image edge, click where the corner WOULD be — including outside the image, on the grey margin. Those coordinates are stored unclamped. Flag it O and/or C. Winding is normalised for you (clockwise from the top-left corner), so click the four corners in any rotational order as long as you go around the quad, not across it — a quad that comes out crossed is drawn red with a "!". Label back-to-front so z comes out right.
